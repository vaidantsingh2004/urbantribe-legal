Privacy Policy

What URBAN TRIBE collects, why, who can see it, where it is stored, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it.

  • Effective date: 1 December 2026
  • Operator: Vaidant Singh, Gurgaon, Haryana, India

In short

  • URBAN TRIBE is for adults (18+) in India to meet people and make small plans together.
  • You sign in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Others see your profile (name, age, city or town, photos, interests, bio). They never see your email, date of birth or private answers.
  • We don't collect your GPS location, contacts or payment details, and URBAN TRIBE shows no ads.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1What we collect

When you sign in

URBAN TRIBE uses Sign in with Google. Google shares your email address, name and profile picture link with our sign-in provider (Supabase). We use your email address to identify your account and never show it to anyone.

Your profile, seen by other members who are allowed to see it

  • Display name, bio and interests.
  • The city or town you choose, and for some large cities a broad area (for example "Bandra, Mumbai"). Never an exact address.
  • Your age, calculated from your date of birth. The date itself is never shown.
  • Profile photos. Every profile needs at least one. Photos are chosen from your phone's gallery only (the app never opens the camera) and are re-encoded on your phone before upload, which removes hidden details such as GPS location and camera information.
  • Your badge and number of completed meetups.

Private information, only you can see it

  • Date of birth: used to confirm you are 18 or older and to show your age. It can't be changed in the app.
  • How you identify (gender), whether you are part of the LGBTQ+ community (optional), and who you would like to meet in Discover.
  • Privacy settings, such as whether you appear in Discover or on the leaderboard.

What you do in URBAN TRIBE

  • Plans you create (title, activity, venue name, area, time and description), join requests, likes and passes, matches, chats and messages, and meetup confirmations.
  • Blocks and reports you make.
  • In-app notifications we create for you, for example "New join request". They never contain the text of your messages.

Venue search and maps

When you search for a meeting place, your search text (and, if you picked a city, that city's centre point) is sent to Google Places, and maps are shown with Google Maps. URBAN TRIBE does not ask for or collect your device location.

Error reports

If the app hits an unexpected error, it sends us the type of error, the places in URBAN TRIBE's own code where it happened, the app version and whether the phone runs Android or iOS. Error reports contain no message text, no name, no email and no account or device identifier.

Technical data

Our service providers receive standard technical data with each request (such as IP address and basic device and app information) to run and secure the service.

What we don't collect

Your contacts, payment details, precise location or advertising identifiers.

2Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and confirm you are an adult.
  • To show your profile, plans and messages to the people you interact with.
  • To suggest people and plans by shared interests, your chosen city or town and your Discover choices.
  • To tell you about activity on your account through in-app notifications.
  • To keep the community safe: blocks, reports, and review of reports by our moderators, including suspending or banning accounts.
  • To find and fix problems in the app.

We process your data with your consent, which you give when you create your account and agree to this policy, and for the purposes you would reasonably expect from a social meetup app. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

3Who can see what

  • Other signed-in members see your public profile when they are allowed to: in Discover, as a plan host, or as a member of the same plan or chat.
  • Blocking: people you block, and people who block you, can't see your profile, photos or plans. A block also takes a guest out of the other person's upcoming plans and their chat.
  • Photos are stored privately and shown only through links that expire after about 10 minutes.
  • Plan guests: the host and guests of a plan can see each other and use the plan chat.
  • Nobody else sees your email, date of birth, gender, LGBTQ+ answer or Discover choices.
  • Our moderators can see reported content (for example a reported message) to review a report. Every moderator action is recorded with the moderator's name.

5How long we keep it

  • Your account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, photos, private information, plans you host, messages you sent, likes, matches, join requests and notifications.
  • Kept for safety: reports you made and reports about you are kept, with your account no longer linked to them.
  • Error reports are deleted after 90 days.
  • Backups may hold deleted data for up to 14 days before they are overwritten.

See Delete your account for how deletion works.

6Your rights and choices

  • See and correct your information in the app (Me → Edit Profile, Photos and Settings).
  • Hide yourself from Discover and the leaderboard, and block or report people.
  • Delete your account in the app (Me → Settings → Delete Account) or by email, as described on the account deletion page.
  • Ask us about the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, or nominate someone to act for you, by writing to urbantribe.admin@gmail.com.

9Security

Access to data is enforced by the database itself (row-level security), photos are kept in private storage behind expiring links, connections are encrypted, and moderator access is limited and logged. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
