Delete your URBAN TRIBE account

You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent.

1Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open URBAN TRIBE and sign in.
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm that you understand it is permanent.
  4. Tap Confirm with Google and choose the same Google account you use for URBAN TRIBE.
  5. Type DELETE to finish.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

2Can't open the app? Request deletion by email

Email urbantribe.admin@gmail.com from the Gmail address you use for URBAN TRIBE, with the subject "Delete my URBAN TRIBE account".

  • We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.
  • We delete the account within 7 days and reply when it is done.
Email a deletion request

3What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, date of birth and private settings.
  • Your likes, matches, join requests and notifications.
  • Plans you host (they disappear for people who joined them).
  • Messages you sent (they disappear from other people's chats too).
  • Your sign-in with URBAN TRIBE.

4What is kept, and for how long

  • Reports you made and reports about you are kept for safety review, with your account no longer linked to them.
  • Backups may still contain deleted data for up to 14 days before they are overwritten.
  • App error reports never contain your account, so there is nothing of yours in them to delete. They are removed after 90 days.

5After deletion

  • Deletion can't be undone.
  • You can sign up again later with the same Google account, as a new account.
  • You can also remove URBAN TRIBE's access from your Google account: myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Questions? Write to urbantribe.admin@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
